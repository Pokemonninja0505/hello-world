## 4. Macro & Market Themes

**Theme 1: The Long-End Bond Market Is the Market's Real Boss**

For most of 2026, equity investors ignored the bond market's creeping stress. That is no longer tenable. The 30-year at 5.33% before today's Treasury intervention is not a data point — it is a regime signal. Long-duration assets (tech stocks, growth equities, long-dated bonds) face a structural multiple compression until either (a) yields reverse decisively, (b) earnings growth accelerates materially beyond current forecasts, or (c) the Fed capitulates and commits to a dovish pivot. None of those conditions are present today. The Treasury buyback is a stopgap, not a reversal. We are early in the repricing of the risk-free rate's impact on equity valuations.

**Theme 2: The AI CapEx Bubble Is Being Tested**

The AI infrastructure trade that dominated 2024–2025 is encountering its first serious skepticism test. Alphabet's H1 2026 capex of $98B and its negative free cash flow quarter have crystallized investor concern about payback timelines. This is not a thesis-killer — AI adoption is real — but it is an inflection point in the narrative. The market is moving from "buy anything AI-adjacent" to "show me the returns." Companies with clear AI monetization paths (cloud pricing, enterprise software, inference revenue) will decouple from pure infrastructure plays. Semi equipment and memory (the picks-and-shovels trade) are under the most pressure because they are furthest from the end-user revenue.
