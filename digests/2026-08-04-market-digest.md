## 2. Top News & Catalysts

**1. Trump Cancels Iran Strike, Opens Door to Talks — Oil Crashes, Stocks Surge [Major]**

On Monday August 3, Trump called off a planned military strike on Iran and said he was pursuing a negotiated deal to halt Tehran's nuclear program and reopen the Strait of Hormuz. WTI dropped 5-6% in a single session ($83-88 range down to the high $70s), its largest one-day move in months. Equity futures surged in tandem. The twin effect: oil at $76-81 acts as a disinflation gift to consumers and the Fed, while AI and tech earnings look better against lower energy input costs. Positioning implication is directional — short oil against deal progress, overweight risk assets if ceasefire holds. Critical caveat: Iran denied that direct talks are underway. Trump called this Tehran's "final opportunity." This is a ceasefire in messaging, not a signed agreement. The relief rally is real; the underlying resolution is not.

**2. Palantir Q2 2026 Blowout — US Commercial AI Revenue +149% YoY [Major]**

Palantir reported Monday AH: EPS $0.41 adjusted vs. $0.35 expected; revenue $1.94B vs. $1.80B expected. Revenue grew 93% year-over-year. US commercial segment grew 149% YoY. Full-year 2026 US commercial revenue guidance raised to "in excess of $3.42B" from prior guide of $3.22B. Stock surged 12-16% in Tuesday premarket. This is the first clean datapoint of the earnings season directly validating enterprise AI ROI at scale. Palantir is not a speculative AI name — it is executing on contract deployments with measurable revenue acceleration. The implication for AMD, NVDA, and the AI stack broadly: if the software layer is monetizing this fast, the infrastructure underneath stays in demand.

**3. AMD Q2 2026 Earnings After the Bell — The Report Card on AI Infrastructure [High]**

AMD reports Q2 tonight after market close. Consensus: revenue $11.34B (+47% YoY), EPS $1.61 adjusted. Data center segment expected at $6.5B, more than doubling from Q2 2025 ($3.2B). The key watch is the Anthropic chip partnership and MI300X/MI350 traction. Options are pricing an 8.5% move in either direction; AMD closed Monday at $484.64, implying a post-earnings range of ~$440-$522. A beat here following Palantir's blowout would confirm the AI revenue cycle is accelerating across the stack — from software to silicon.

**4. SpaceX First-Ever Public Earnings Report — Binary Event With Lockup Overhang [High]**

SpaceX (SPCX) reports after the close tonight — its first quarterly earnings as a public company. Revenue estimate: $6.88B. The company now spans space, connectivity (Starlink), and AI following the xAI/X integration in February 2026. EPS consensus ranges from -$1.26 to +$0.33 — one of the widest spreads relative to company size on record, reflecting genuine uncertainty about AI capex allocation and Starlink profitability trajectory. Stock closed Monday at $114, down 46% from its June 16 high of $211. Lock-up expiration is August 6, two trading days away. Even a strong report faces structural selling pressure from early investors. Risk/reward is asymmetric and adversarial heading into tonight.

**5. Copper Surges on US Tariff Front-Running — Demand Signal Distorted [Moderate]**

Copper reached ~$14,000/ton LME and $6.55/lb COMEX, near two-month highs. The driver is not Chinese demand or global manufacturing growth — it is US tariff arbitrage. Over 200,000 tons arrived at US ports in July, the largest monthly volume in shipping data history, sapping availability elsewhere. The traditional copper-as-global-demand-indicator signal is compromised for the near term. Real demand growth is unclear; the metal is being stockpiled in US warehouses before tariff implementation.

**6. Caterpillar Joins the Rally [Lower]**

CAT shares jumped Tuesday alongside industrials catching a bid. Lower oil reduces input costs, and AI-driven infrastructure spending supports equipment demand. No confirmed earnings beat; the move appears correlated with the broader market rather than company-specific. Worth monitoring as a proxy for whether the industrial/capex cycle is extending.

## 4. Macro & Market Themes

**Theme 1: The Iran-Oil-Stocks Trade — Geopolitical Relief Is Not Resolution**

The market is behaving as if the Iran situation is settled. It is not. Trump called off a strike; Iran denied direct talks; the Strait of Hormuz remains a pressure point. The 5-6% oil crash on Monday priced in a meaningful probability of a sustained ceasefire and deal. If talks stall or collapse, oil snaps back sharply and stocks reverse a significant portion of this week's gains. The market is long a deal that has not been signed.

The correct frame: this is not a fundamental shift in oil supply. It is a reprieve in geopolitical risk premium. Iranian nuclear capability is unchanged. The correct position is to treat the oil short as real and tradeable only as long as diplomatic signals hold, and to maintain active stops sized for a fast reversal. Every day talks fail to produce a framework, the probability of re-escalation rises.

**Theme 2: AI Earnings Super-Cycle — First Confirmation, Not Conclusion**

Palantir's 93% revenue growth and 149% US commercial surge is the first clean validation of enterprise AI ROI in this earnings cycle. The market spent July skeptical — too much capex, not enough revenue. Palantir answered that directly. AMD tonight is the infrastructure-layer equivalent of the same question. If AMD's data center comes in above $6.5B and the company raises, that is a two-data-point confirmation that the AI revenue cycle is accelerating across the full stack. Two beats in sequence on consecutive nights would restart the AI trade that was sold off through July, from a base where positioning is not crowded. The entry here is not chasing — the July selloff washed out the momentum longs. It is repositioning into a confirmed earnings cycle.
