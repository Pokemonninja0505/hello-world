Positioning implication: energy sector has a fundamental earnings tailwind that persists even as rate pressure weighs on growth names.

## 4. Macro & Market Themes

**Theme 1: The July Hold Is Being Reprocessed**
The Fed held rates at 3.50%–3.75% in July as inflation data was softening. The August NFP at 162,000 — three times consensus — is now forcing the market to reconsider whether that hold was a mistake. We are in the middle of that repricing. The 10Y at 4.78%, DXY at 99.157, and gold's knee-jerk sell are all expressions of the same trade. This repricing is not finished — it runs until CPI on September 11 either confirms or kills it. We are mid-trade, not early. The risk to the short-bond side is a cold CPI print triggering a violent short-cover rally.

**Theme 2: Iran-Driven Energy Inflation as a Structural Fed Constraint**
The $90+ oil story is not transitory — it is the result of an active conflict disrupting a major oil-producing region. Supply-side energy inflation does not respond to rate hikes. The Fed cannot drill oil. This creates structural tension: hiking suppresses demand-side growth but cannot address the root cause of inflation. Energy equities benefit directly — their earnings grow with oil while the rest of the market suffers tighter conditions. This dynamic can persist through multiple FOMC meetings.
