## 2. Top News & Catalysts

**1. Strait of Hormuz - Iranian Demands Cloud Reopening Timeline (HIGH IMPACT)**
Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi stated over the weekend that while Iran and Oman are "close to an agreement," the waterway will not reopen until Washington eases sanctions on Tehran and pays war reparations. Shipping through the strait - which carried roughly 20% of global oil supply before the conflict began in late February - has effectively collapsed, with only 8-15 vessel transits in recent days versus ~130 before the war. The IEA has called this the "largest supply disruption in the history of the global oil market." Today's oil bounce reflects the market correctly reading Iranian demands as maximalist and a near-term deal as implausible. Positioning implication: the energy risk premium in oil is structural, not temporary; geopolitical shorts in oil are dangerous while these demands stand.

**2. Shopify Reports Blowout Q2 - AI Monetization Confirmed (HIGH IMPACT)**
SHOP surged +17% to $144.24 (from prior close of $123.30) on Q2 results: revenue $3.58B (+33.7% YoY), GMV $115.57B (+32%). Management attributed the acceleration to AI integration tripling customer traffic and orders, while affirming that AI is additive to margins, not dilutive. This is the proof point the market has been waiting for - a scaled software company showing AI generates measurable, profitable revenue growth at the application layer. Analyst upgrades flooded in: Morgan Stanley initiated Overweight at $192, BofA reinstated Buy at $150, Jefferies upgraded to Buy at $160, Stifel at $150. Positioning implication: confirms the AI application layer thesis, which is early in its re-rating. Names with real AI revenue integration - not just AI investment spend - should re-rate higher.

**3. JPMorgan Raises Year-End S&P 500 Target to 8,000 (MEDIUM-HIGH IMPACT)**
JPMorgan's Dubravko Lakos-Bujas team lifted their S&P 500 year-end target to 8,000 from 7,800 - their second raise in two months. The team cited: AI capex translating into real earnings, revised EPS to $365 for 2026 and $420 for 2027 (from $350/$390), and 78% of reporting companies beating EPS with 73% beating revenue. At least seven brokerages now share the 8,000 view. Positioning implication: sell-side consensus is bullish and targets are being raised as the market approaches them - classic late-cycle confirmation, but not yet a contrarian sell signal when earnings are still beating.

**4. AMD Posts Record Quarter, Stock Volatile (MEDIUM IMPACT)**
AMD reported Q2 revenue of $11.54B (record), beating estimates, but fell ~8% in after-hours on earnings day on free cash flow concerns and high investor expectations following a strong pre-earnings rally. By today's session, AMD recovered sharply to ~$524.25 (+9.16%) as dip buyers stepped in and analysts raised targets - Argus to $625 (from $450), Rosenblatt to $700 (from $665), consensus at $613. The setup reads as: earnings quality is strong, the FCF concern was overblown, and the post-earnings dip was a buy-the-gap opportunity. The move back above prior resistance at $486 is technically constructive.

**5. CPI Wednesday - The Hinge (MEDIUM IMPACT, HIGH FORWARD RELEVANCE)**
July CPI releases Wednesday, August 12 at 8:30 AM ET. This is the first major inflation print since the Iran-conflict energy shock. The 10Y yield declining to 4.651% and VIX at 14.9 signal the market is positioned for a benign outcome. The binary is wide: cool print = S&P pushes toward 7,900, rates fall, small caps extend; hot print = rates rise, multiple compression, 2-3% drawdown from ATH.

## 4. Macro & Market Themes

**Theme 1: The Iran Discount is Permanent Until It Isn't**
The market has been living with the Hormuz closure since late February - roughly five months. The equity market has largely looked through it (S&P at ATH), but the commodity complex has not: oil at $78-84, gold at $4,300+, copper near record highs. This divergence is sustainable only as long as the Iran risk remains "priced in but contained." Iranian Foreign Minister demands (sanctions relief plus war reparations) suggest no near-term resolution. The risk is a binary: if a deal is announced, oil flushes 15-20% immediately, inflation expectations drop, and the Fed narrative shifts dovish - that is unambiguously equity bullish but creates violent sector rotation away from energy. If talks collapse further, oil re-tests $90+, CPI gets a new energy shock, and the Fed has to re-evaluate its on-hold posture. We are in an unstable equilibrium that appears stable.

**Theme 2: AI Revenue Is Arriving - The Rotation Trade Begins**
Shopify's quarter is the clearest evidence yet that AI is not just a cost center or a hype story - it is generating measurable, high-margin revenue growth at the application layer. This follows a string of AI infrastructure wins (hyperscalers, Nvidia). The market trade from here is rotation from pure AI infrastructure names (which are priced for perfection) to AI-enabled software that is only beginning to see multiple re-expansion. Shopify moved 17% in one session. The sector broadly has not moved yet. Early innings of the AI app layer re-rating is the highest-conviction multi-week theme in this market.
