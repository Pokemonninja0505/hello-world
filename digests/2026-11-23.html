Section 4
Macro & Market Themes

Theme 1: AI Valuation Reset — Inning 3 of 9

The Anthropic IPO was the catalyst, but the underlying issue is fundamental: what multiple do you put on AI earnings that may not materialize for 2–3 years? The AI trade from early 2025 through October 2026 was driven by two things — Nvidia revenue beats and the assumption that hyperscaler AI capex would translate to outsized returns. Neither has been tested against a real tightening cycle. The December Fed hike — almost certainly coming — is the first real rate test for this trade. The sell-off may pause on Monday (oversold bounce), but the valuation reset needs either credible AI earnings delivery or a Fed pivot to get back to highs. Neither is coming in the next two weeks. We are in the middle innings of this correction, not the end.

Theme 2: Fed Re-Enters as a Negative Catalyst

Markets spent most of 2026 pricing in a gradual rate-cut cycle. That is now fully reversed. With 99.2% probability on a December hike and 10Y yields near 5%, the discount rate that drove AI multiple expansion is now a headwind. This mirrors the late-2022 dynamic that crushed high-multiple tech — just with different leadership names. Key difference from 2022: corporate balance sheets are stronger, and actual AI revenues (Nvidia, Microsoft, Google) are real and growing. That sets a floor. But it does not prevent a further 5–10% compression in price-to-sales multiples for the most expensive names before December 9.

Bottom Line

What mattered today was not Monday’s bounce — it was the context beneath it: the market entered correction territory on an AI valuation crisis compounded by near-certain Fed rate hike expectations, with the Anthropic IPO fizzle as the symbolic event that broke the AI bull narrative.

The next 3–5 days are likely to see continued oversold relief, with futures-led gaps higher that fade as each session progresses, as the market seeks a sustainable bottom before the December 9 FOMC decision.

The risk that breaks the thesis entirely is an ANT break below $83 combined with Nvidia earnings disappointment — if both happen before December 9, the AI sector could see another leg down of 10–15% that neither institutional buyers nor oversold conditions can absorb.
