Following Tuesday's below-consensus CPI (3.4% annual, +0.1% monthly), Thursday's PPI came in materially softer than expected — YoY 4.7% vs 5.5% prior, Core PPI at 4.2%. The Fed does not meet until September, so PCE (due August 29) becomes the critical next data point. Markets now price less than one full hike in 2026. This is the single most important macro catalyst of the week: it drove Thursday's record S&P 500 at 7,800 and is anchoring the rate-cut narrative that is fueling the Russell 2000 outperformance today. Positioning implication: bullish equities broadly, bullish long-duration bonds, bearish USD, bullish rate-sensitive sectors (small caps, utilities, real estate).

**2. Reddit (RDDT) Added to S&P 500 — Effective August 18**
S&P Dow Jones Indices announced RDDT replaces AvalonBay Communities (AVB) in the S&P 500, effective before the market open on August 18. JPMorgan estimates index funds must purchase approximately 16.7 million RDDT shares — nearly 3x the stock's average daily trading volume since its March 2024 debut. Stock surged 14.48% to ~$176.25 on Aug 14. RDDT is down 31.2% YTD, creating a volatile base. Positioning implication: forced buying creates a mechanical bid into Monday's open. But S&P 500 additions historically exhibit a "sell the event" pattern post-rebalance — the index-driven buying exhausts into the close of the effective date. Monitor for a post-Aug-18 short setup.

**3. Alphabet (GOOGL) -2.4% — DOJ Considering Full Structural Breakup**
Reports that the Department of Justice is pursuing a full-scale breakup of Alphabet following the court ruling that its payments to Apple and Samsung to be the default search engine constituted an illegal monopoly. Stock at ~$360, rebounding from the July 24 post-earnings low of $315.04, but today's DOJ headline halts that recovery. The Q2 fundamentals are genuinely strong: $119.8B revenue (+24%), Cloud at $24.8B (+82%), 35.6% cloud margin. The stock trades at its lowest P/E since 2019 (17.87x trailing) against 24% top-line growth. The legal process for a structural breakup is years long, but headline risk is elevated and the timing is uncertain. Positioning implication: GOOGL is a fundamental buy at current levels for long-duration holders. Short-term momentum is against it.

**4. Nu Holdings (NU) +13% After Record Q2 Earnings**
NU crossed $1B in quarterly net income for the first time: $5.9B revenue (beat $5.48B consensus), 33% ROE, accelerating expansion across Brazil, Mexico, and Colombia. **5. SanDisk (SNDK) Investor Day — NAND Supercycle Call, Stock +7-13% Over Two Days**

The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index is down ~24% since late June. SanDisk's NAND supercycle call today introduces a potential re-entry point for the chip layer — but the key test is whether AI ROI is now visible in earnings (it is at NU, it is at AMAT, it is at GOOGL Cloud) versus being a promise priced on expectations. The rotation is not over — mid-cycle, money is moving upstack to where AI revenue flows into P&L margin expansion.
