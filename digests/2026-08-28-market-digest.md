## 2. Top News & Catalysts

**1. Jackson Hole: Warsh Signals Rate Hikes Coming — September Now Above 50%** *(Dominant event)*

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh delivered his first Jackson Hole speech Friday and surprised markets with the sharpest hawkish pivot of his tenure. He called inflation "the Fed's biggest problem," stated it remains above the 2% target, and issued the clearest hike signal yet: "We must be confident that underlying inflation is moving to our objective, clearly and at sufficient speed. Otherwise, we have work to do." He added that while summer inflation readings were "better than expected, they do not tell me that underlying trends have meaningfully improved."

Warsh also broke from recent Fed communication norms, explicitly dialing back forward guidance and telling market participants not to look to the Fed for "their next trade." Before the speech, September rate hike odds were roughly 33%. After, they moved above 50% and the 10Y yield climbed to 4.73%.

*Positioning implication:* This is the dominant macro event heading into September. Every rate-sensitive trade reprices from here. Underweight small caps, REITs, utilities. Dollar strengthens. The entire "soft landing / rate cut" consensus is under pressure.

## 4. Macro & Market Themes

**Theme 1: AI Earnings Ceiling Has Not Been Hit — But the Rate Ceiling Might Have**

NVDA plus CRM constitute the third major earnings cycle confirming that AI infrastructure and enterprise AI spending are in aggressive growth mode. The bear thesis — "AI capex gets cut as CFOs face margin pressure" — took another direct hit. Elastic adds a third data point. The AI trade is not crowded; it is still broadly underowned by institutional allocators who have been waiting for proof of revenue, which they now have.

But the tension heading into September is this: these are long-duration, high-multiple assets. Warsh's repricing of September hike odds above 50% introduces a discount rate problem that earnings beats can only partially offset. The market's inability to produce an index-level rally Friday despite the NVDA and CRM beats is the canary — the macro regime just shifted. The question for the next 3-5 weeks is whether earnings momentum can stay ahead of the rate headwind, or whether the rate path becomes the dominant variable and forces a multiple compression cycle even in the AI winners.

**Theme 2: Gold's -3.18% Decline on Inflation Day Is a Cross-Asset Signal**

Gold is supposed to be the inflation hedge. Warsh just confirmed inflation is still too high. Gold sold off 3.18%. The explanation is internally consistent: gold is already priced for elevated inflation (+29.13% YoY). What it is now pricing is whether the Fed *successfully beats inflation back*, which is dollar-bullish and real-rates-bullish — both gold-bearish. If the market believes the hike will work, gold underperforms. If the hike fails to tame inflation, gold's next leg is sharply higher. This binary is the most important macro setup heading into September. The current $4,454 level is effectively the market pricing a successful Fed response to inflation.
