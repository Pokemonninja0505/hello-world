## 4. Macro & Market Themes

**Theme 1: Oil as a Policy Constraint — The Stagflation Squeeze**
The US-Iran war has created a structural floor under energy prices that the Fed cannot ignore. Oil at $95-100 adds directly to headline CPI, which is already at 3.4%. The Fed's mandate forces them to respond to inflation even when the cause is a supply shock — they cannot cut to support growth without risking an inflation re-acceleration. The result is a market that faces both economic slowdown risk (from oil costs squeezing consumers and businesses) and monetary tightening risk simultaneously. We are in the early innings of this dynamic — the conflict escalation has been building since February 2026 and is not priced as a tail risk; it is now the baseline. The question is how much further it goes. The $100 level on WTI is not just symbolic — above it, airline, trucking, and consumer discretionary earnings estimates require downward revision at scale. We are one session away from that threshold.

**Theme 2: The Semis Rotation — From AI Hype Premium to Execution Premium**
The INTC/AMD vs. NVDA divergence on Tuesday is not noise; it is a regime signal. For 18 months, the market paid any multiple for AI exposure regardless of execution. The Terafab announcement and AMD's raised TAM outlook are both concrete — specific manufacturing commitments and measurable addressable market projections. The market rewarded specificity. NVDA remains the dominant hardware platform, but its forward P/E has disconnected from near-term earnings in a way that becomes harder to justify as rates rise. The rotation is early and may not persist, but it is worth tracking: if INTC holds above its breakout and NVDA fails to recover its Tuesday loss, the regime shift has legs. This matters for index construction — semis are a large weight in the Nasdaq, and how the sub-sector allocates determines Nasdaq directionality near-term.
