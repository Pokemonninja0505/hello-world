## 2. Top News & Catalysts

**1. AI CIRCULAR FINANCING FEAR — DOMINANT MARKET NARRATIVE**

Fresh concerns that hyperscalers have been funding AI capital expenditure through circular equity arrangements — co-investments with chip manufacturers that inflate stated capex figures without representing genuine end-demand for chips. If these structures unwind or hyperscalers scale back, the demand assumptions baked into chip stock valuations look overstated by a significant margin. Samsung and SK Hynix fell 13.4% and 14.7% in Korea on Monday. Micron dropped ~13% in a prior session; AMD 7–8%. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) has already given back a substantial portion of its prior 130% run. Positioning implication: semis are not bottomed until either the thesis is disproven by hyperscaler earnings guidance or the narrative exhausts itself on capitulation volume. Do not aggressively buy the dip without a catalyst.

**2. US-IRAN ATTACK PAUSE — OIL AND EQUITIES CATALYST**

The US and Iran agreed to a temporary pause in military exchanges. Stocks rose in early Tuesday trading; oil pulled back from $93.45. This is the immediate catalyst for the Tuesday equity futures bounce. The key qualifier is "temporary" — every prior de-escalation in this conflict since the summer escalation has been followed by resumed hostilities. The Strait of Hormuz remains the choke point. Do not press bearish oil or bullish equity trades assuming the pause is durable beyond 72 hours without confirmation.

**3. FOMC MEETING BEGINS — DECISION WEDNESDAY**

The Federal Reserve opened its two-day July 28–29 meeting today. Current target rate: 3.5–3.75%. Market pricing: 62% probability of no change, down from 80% a week ago. The market is not pricing a hike; the residual 38% probability reflects hawkish tail risk. The real market event is Wednesday's press conference — specifically whether Powell signals any September cut optionality or doubles down on "higher for longer" language. This is the most important near-term market catalyst. Everything else today is positioning ahead of Wednesday.

**4. EARNINGS — IQVIA (+8%), SHERWIN-WILLIAMS (+6.7%)**

IQVIA Holdings reported a Q2 beat with record net new bookings and raised full-year guidance — premarket up ~8%. Sherwin-Williams reported a Q2 beat and raised guidance — up 6.7%. Both are significant as confirmation that the broader economy is not collapsing under the weight of persistent rates. IQVIA is a CRO (pharmaceutical R&D proxy); SHW is a housing and commercial activity proxy. Both raises in guidance signal end-market demand is holding. This is incrementally bullish for the broader market narrative even as tech AI names are under structural pressure.

**5. SPACEX (SPCX) — POST-IPO DETERIORATION**

SpaceX IPO'd June 12 at $135, hit $225.60, and is now at $113.50 — 16% below its IPO price, 50%+ from the peak. Eight consecutive sessions below the IPO price. Earnings due August 4 trigger a staggered lock-up expiry — the first window for insiders to begin selling. Positioning implication: the stock faces a supply overhang heading into August 4 regardless of earnings quality. If earnings disappoint, the lock-up selling adds a second leg down. Watch the $100 level as the next psychological floor.

**6. PMI DATA — RESILIENT AT 53–54**

Both services and manufacturing PMI came in at 53–54, signaling continued expansion. This is the data that is holding the equity market up and simultaneously holding the Fed in place. Strong PMI = no emergency rate cut needed = rate-sensitive growth stocks don't get valuation relief. It's incrementally bearish for the argument that the Fed needs to pivot.

## 4. Macro & Market Themes

**Theme 1: AI Chip Selloff — Middle Innings, Not the End**

The AI chip trade was built on assumptions of uninterrupted hyperscaler capex growth. The circular financing narrative is not a confirmed fact, but it has introduced structural doubt into those assumptions. The Korea selloff (KOSPI -10.84%) is the market's most honest signal — memory chip manufacturers have the most direct exposure to whether AI hardware demand is real or circular. The prior 130% rally in the SOX means there is a lot of air beneath these stocks even after the recent correction. We are in the middle innings of the rethink: the narrative has been introduced, but it has not been either confirmed (via hyperscaler capex cuts) or disproven (via affirmation in earnings). The resolution comes in the next two to three weeks as Microsoft, Amazon, Google, and Meta report. Until then, the sector is in a state of suspended uncertainty — bounces are tactical, not durable.

**Theme 2: FOMC Hold + Goldilocks Fading**

The market entered 2026 pricing in multiple rate cuts. It has not gotten them. Inflation has remained above 2% for five consecutive years. The PMI at 53–54 confirms the economy does not need emergency relief. This is Goldilocks fading — not a recession, but not a cut either. In this environment, the valuation expansion that drove the prior rally is capped. Stocks that grow earnings the old-fashioned way (IQVIA, SHW, consumer staples with pricing power) outperform. Speculative AI infrastructure plays without near-term earnings power underperform. The rotation is already visible in today's price action: Dow +0.51% (value/defensives), Nasdaq -0.18% (growth/tech). This rotation has legs as long as the 10Y stays above 4.50%.
