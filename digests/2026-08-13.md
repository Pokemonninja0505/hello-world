## 4. Macro & Market Themes

**Theme 1: September Cut Inflection — Rate Sensitives Lead Until the Data Turns**

The market is at 87.2% implied probability for a September cut, built on two pillars: a net job loss in July and cooling inflation that is now at 2.5% core YoY. This is a structural inflection for rate-sensitive assets — the Russell 2000 has led the "Great Rotation" of 2026 for this reason. Financials and industrials constitute over 40% of the index's value-tilted composition; both sectors benefit from lower borrowing costs and domestic growth expectations. The trade is past the "discovery" phase (it became consensus in Q1), which means it is also more fragile. The September cut is not guaranteed — one hot inflation print in August reverses 87.2% pricing sharply and violently punishes small caps. We are at the crowded-but-not-exhausted stage: the thesis is intact, but the stop is well-defined and nearby.

**Theme 2: Networking Supercycle — Confirmed by Orders, Not Narratives**

Cisco's +35% order growth is qualitatively different from management forward guidance or analyst estimates — it represents capital already committed by customers across every geography and market. This is the strongest confirmation yet that AI infrastructure spending is in full cycle acceleration. Combined with AVGO's +143% AI accelerator revenue and hyperscaler capex disclosures not showing signs of deceleration, the AI infrastructure buildout appears to be in early-to-mid innings, not late stage. The risk to this thesis is a cyclical inventory surge being misread as a structural cycle — but Cisco's cross-customer, cross-geography breadth argues against a single-buyer distortion. The supercycle trade has fundamental backing that did not exist six months ago.
