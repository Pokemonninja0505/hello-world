## 4. Macro & Market Themes

**Theme 1: The AI Capex Reckoning — Midcycle Reset or Start of the Unwind?**
The market has been pricing AI infrastructure as a one-way growth story for 18 months. CoreWeave's debt distress and Anthropic's decelerating revenue growth are the first visible cracks in the monetization thesis. This is not 2000 — the technology is real and enterprise adoption is happening. But the *financing structure* of AI build-out (GPU leasing, leveraged datacenters, perpetual capex) is being stress-tested by a 5%+ 10-year yield. The analogy is telecom capex in 1999-2000: the infrastructure eventually mattered, but investors who bought at peak financing multiples lost badly before recovering. We are early in the repricing of that financing risk, not late. The trade — rotting out of the infrastructure/GPU-rental layer and into model companies or pure-play enterprise software beneficiaries — is still in early innings.

**Theme 2: Stagflationary Pinch — The Fed's Worst Scenario**
PPI at 6.3% and oil at $108–110 is a supply-shock inflation profile, not a demand-pull one. The Fed cannot cure Hormuz closures with rate hikes. But it also cannot ignore headline inflation at 3.7% and core above target. The market has re-priced to a December hike as the base case; the real question is whether the FOMC sees enough demand weakness by October 27–28 to hold. If they hike in October despite the data-dependent framing, it would be a significant hawkish surprise. The 10-year at 5.08% reflects this uncertainty. Growth equities — especially unprofitable ones — are structurally challenged at these yield levels.
